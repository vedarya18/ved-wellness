Delete your Ved Wellness account

You can delete your account and all your data at any time. Choose either way below.

Option 1: In the app (instant)

  1. Open Ved Wellness and log in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account and data and confirm.

Option 2: Without the app (by email)

Send us an email from the address you used to sign up. We will delete your account within 7 days and confirm by email.

Email a delete request

Or write to vedwellness.app@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Ved Wellness account".

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login
  • Your profile details: mobile, age, height, weights, goals
  • All your daily logs (weight, sleep, steps)
  • Your body checkup readings and session attendance
  • Your profile photo and your private progress photos
  • Personal notes from your coach and your phone's notification address
  • Your consent records and membership details

Copies in system backups are removed automatically within 30 days. Deletion cannot be undone.
