Ved Wellness

The companion app for members of Wellness Coach Ved Arya

Ved Wellness helps coaching members build healthy daily habits. Members track their weight goal, sleep and steps, join live coaching sessions on Zoom, and get guidance from their coach.

Members only. To use the app you need an invite code from your coach. Your coach approves your membership.

Important

Ved Wellness is a coaching and habit-tracking tool. It does not give medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Please consult a doctor before changing your diet, exercise or medicines.
