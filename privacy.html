Privacy Policy

Version 5 · Effective 1 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Ved Wellness app collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. Ved Wellness is run by Ved Arya (Wellness Coach Ved Arya), India ("we", "us").

1. Data we collect

DataWhy we use it
Name, email address, passwordTo create and secure your account. Your password is stored by our login system in encrypted form; nobody, including your coach, can read it.
Mobile numberSo your coach can contact you about your coaching (for example on WhatsApp or by call).
Gender, age, height, starting weight, target weightTo set your goals, work out your BMI and show your progress.
Your goal, activity level, diet type, wellness goals and the meal plan you followSo your coach can plan guidance that suits you.
Daily logs: weight, sleep hours, steps and the meals of your plan you tick as done (with dates)To show your progress, streaks and badges, and to let your coach guide you.
Step count from your phone's motion sensorTo count your daily steps. The raw sensor data stays on your phone; only the daily total is saved.
Profile photo (optional)To show your picture in the app.
Progress photos (optional)So you can compare your own change over time. They are private: only you can see them, not your coach.
Personal notes your coach writes for youTo give you one-to-one guidance. Only you and your coach can see them.
A push notification address for your phone (a random token, not your number)To send you alerts such as a new message or note from your coach, your approval, or a new post. You can turn notifications off in your phone settings.
Body checkup readings added by you or your coach: weight, body fat, muscle, visceral fat, resting metabolism, body age, body water (with dates and notes)To track your body composition over time and guide your coaching.
Live session attendance (when you tap Join)So you and your coach can see how regularly you attend.
Result stories (before/after photo, name, story)Shown to other members only if you give your coach written permission. You can ask for it to be removed at any time.
Wellness Marathon (only if you join): your daily habit check-ins, team and role, kit checklist and your "why"To run the marathon. Other players in the same marathon see your first name, team, role and points. Your weight change, kit checklist and "why" are seen only by you and your coach. Your team Captain and Assistant Captain can see whether you checked in today. You can leave a marathon at any time.
Membership status and end date, consent recordsTo manage your access and record that you agreed to this policy.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or payment details. Payments for coaching happen outside the app.

2. Who can see your data

  • You.
  • Your coach, Ved Arya, after you join with his invite code. He can see your profile details, profile photo and logs so he can guide you. He cannot see your progress photos.
  • Our service providers, only to run the app: Supabase (database, login and file storage, servers in Mumbai, India), Expo (app delivery, updates and push notifications), Google Firebase (delivering push notifications to Android phones) and Google (sending app emails such as password reset codes).

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with advertisers.

3. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, logs and photo. Copies in system backups are removed automatically within 30 days.

4. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you can:

  • see the data we hold about you,
  • correct or update it (most of it directly in the app's Profile tab),
  • delete your account and data (in the app: Profile → Delete my account and data, or on the web),
  • withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account,
  • raise a complaint with us, and if not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

5. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access rules make sure each member sees only their own data, and the coach sees only members linked to him. No system is perfect; if you notice anything wrong, please tell us at once.

6. Age

Ved Wellness is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Not medical advice

The app is for tracking and general wellness guidance only. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a replacement for a doctor.

8. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will show the new version in the app and ask for your agreement again.
