Common questions

I forgot my password. On the login screen tap "Forgot password?", enter your email and use the code we send you.

I am waiting for approval. Your coach approves new members. Message your coach, then tap "Check again" in the app.

My steps are not counting. Allow the app to use physical activity (motion) in your phone settings. You can also add steps manually.

How do I delete my account? See the delete account page.
